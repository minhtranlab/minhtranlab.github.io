Matrices as transformations: the house explorer

Column 1 of the matrix is where the teal arrow (1, 0) goes: the floor of the house. Column 2 is where the coral arrow (0, 1) goes: the left wall. Every other point follows: M(x, y) = x × column 1 + y × column 2.

Where this comes from

Things to try:

  • Rotation: watch the teal tip slide round the circle to (cos θ, sin θ). That is column 1.
  • Reflection: set θ = 45 to get the mirror y = x. The booklet's θ is the mirror's angle, so the matrix uses 2θ.
  • Enlarge + rotate: a = 1, b = 1 gives k = √2 and a 45° turn. Compare with multiplying by 1 + i.
  • Your own matrix: type numbers into the box, or drag the arrow tips.
