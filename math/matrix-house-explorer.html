Matrices as transformations: the house explorer

Column 1 of the matrix is where the teal arrow (1, 0) goes: the floor of the house. Column 2 is where the coral arrow (0, 1) goes: the left wall. Every other point follows: M(x, y) = x × column 1 + y × column 2.

Things to try:

  • Explore → Rotation: the teal tip slides round the circle to (cos θ, sin θ). That is column 1.
  • Two in a row: rotate 90° anticlockwise, then reflect in the x-axis. Now swap the order.
  • Undo: pick "Squash onto the x-axis" and see why it has no inverse.
  • Find the matrix: look where the amber floor and wall went, then type the columns.
  • Show area: the amber shape is the unit square after the move. Its area is |det|.
