## 3. Decisions made (tutor chat, Minh delegated all of them)

1. Numbering: reviews do not use up unit numbers, so Stage 2 "Units 1-3" stays the first three learn units.
2. The 71-Hanzi sentence is cut after the quiet-gesture clause (Unit 6 = 28 Hanzi, Unit 7 = 63 Hanzi). The meaning breaks cleanly there.
3. The no-new-word sentence about the judges being moved starts Unit 4, because Unit 3 already holds 3 sentences.
4. Optional extras added to approved Unit 1 (two collocation rows, the support word 夫妻). Its text and new words are unchanged.
5. RC revisits Units 10-11 and the whole chapter, then teaches 细节 through the title and the story's point. No separate review after Unit 11.
6. Units 9 and 11 stay at 2 words each. Cannot reach 3 inside the rules without a 7-word unit or splitting a quote. Unit 9 is a breather before the 5-word Unit 10; Unit 11 is the payoff sentence.
7. The story's point (my wording, now final): the couple that wins is not the one with the big story or the perfect words; love shows in small details done without being asked. That is why the chapter is called 细节.
8. Display rule: a unit of 3 sentences is shown one sentence per screen, with new words glossed on the screen where they first appear, then the whole unit is shown once to read in one go. This handles the heavy text units (3 and 4: 73 and 74 Hanzi).
9. Review rule: a review samples about 8 words, weakest first. Exam-core words come before story words; story words are still reviewed, but less often.
10. Story words carry the tag "story word" (lower exam priority). Exam-core words are drilled harder.
11. Support words are glossed on screen, never counted, never tested.
12. Split verb-objects (离婚, 吵架) are shown in their split form from the text first, then taught as a pattern.
