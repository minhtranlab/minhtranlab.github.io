## 2. What the app is (product decisions Minh made)

Private, single-page web app that teaches **HSK Standard Course 5A, Chapter 1 "爱的细节"** (Vietnamese edition). It started as dry "one word per screen" drills; he found those mechanical and boring, so the design is now **story-driven and varied**:

- The story IS the book's own sentences (not invented plot). A short invented context is fine only to make vocabulary understandable.
- A study unit = 1–3 consecutive book sentences + about 2–5 new words; about 11–13 screens; lots of illustrations drawn in SVG; as little on screen as possible, extras (pinyin, Hán Việt, etymology/chiết tự + mnemonic, examples, explanation) appear only on tap. No Vietnamese translation visible on a question screen before he answers. No tone drills (the TTS is not good enough for that).
- Interface language is Vietnamese. Works on iPad Safari. Words he taps for a meaning feed the revision list.
- Pinyin appears **only** through the top-right 拼 toggle, as small ruby text above the characters. No extra pinyin lines after answers. No "Đúng rồi" text on a correct answer (the green box is enough).
- Production tasks (order the words, type with a pinyin keyboard), new example sentences, grammar mini-lessons inside some units, and variety of screen types, so it never feels repetitive.
- Ôn tập (mixed review) and 🎬 Ghép truyện placed by the story, plus a separate "Ôn tập tổng hợp" revision path that he can take or skip any day (3/7/21-day spacing). Final test at the end of the chapter. **Study path follows the story** (Minh 01/11, phiếu #5): a 🎬 Ghép truyện right after the last study unit of each complete part of the story (one couple, one paragraph of the book…), covering exactly that part's book sentences; an Ôn tập just before it (words of the units since the last review); a part longer than 3 study units gets one more Ôn tập in the middle (without a Ghép truyện), so there are never more than 3 study units in a row without a review; one Ghép truyện is at most 2 pages (about 10 sentences), a longer part gets two Ghép truyện in a row. Declared in the content as the chapter's `PATH` (Bài 1: `content_path.js`), never computed by a formula.
- **Speaking: one "Nhắc lại" screen (`say`, §6f) in every study unit**, never scored, always skippable (phiếu #5).
- Voices: **female = Matcha (sherpa-onnx `matcha-icefall-zh-baker`, sid 0; the dataset is non-commercial, which Minh accepts for personal/friends use) and male = Kokoro v1.1 voice "D" (sid 70)**. He also likes male "E" (sid 90) and may switch later (about 15 minutes of regeneration). Narration/book lines and word cards use the male voice, question sentences use the female voice (field `v` on each line: `'m'` or `'f'`). He prefers one voice per role.
- Plan: personal use first, then share with a few friends who own the same textbook. A friend will test via the GitHub link and give feedback.

## 5. How to work on it

Requirements in a fresh sandbox: `python3`, `node`, `ffmpeg`, `pip install pypinyin playwright sherpa-onnx soundfile scipy` (Playwright chromium: use the preinstalled one via `executable_path='/opt/pw-browsers/chromium'` if present; do not run `playwright install`). The sandbox network is an allowlist: github.com and package registries work; Google and most sites do not (WebFetch also fails for script.google.com).

1. Edit sources in `hsk5-src/`.
2. `python3 hsk5-src/build.py` → prints vocabulary report, writes `hsk5/index.html` + `hsk5/version.json`, and must end with **`problems: 0`**. It validates every Chinese word in the content and the app against `glossary.txt`: a word must be HSK1–4, or a taught/support word in the glossary; anything else is a problem. Decorative Hán characters in illustrations must also be HSK1–4 or taught words.
3. Tests (from `hsk5-src/tests`): start `python3 serve.py &` (serves `hsk5/` on :8765 with a blank API so tests never touch the real Sheet) and for sync tests `node ../backend/mock_gas.js > /tmp/mock.log &` (fake Apps Script on :8766). Then:
   - `python3 t_rules.py` — the general UI rules of §12 (incl. §12.11 pictures never give the answer away, §12.12 match feedback). Sweeps EVERY question screen (study units, QB, test bank, plus screens generated by buildMix/buildTest/buildRevision/genQ; 277+ screens, all 9 types) on an iPhone viewport 390x844, right answer for all and the wrong/revealed path once per type and source; then a sample per type on iPad 820x1180, including "Tự đọc to" off. ## 6. Engine facts you need before changing things

- State is one object `st` saved to `localStorage` key `hsk5_story_v4` by `save()`; `st.u` = last-modified time. Fields: `sess` (per-session `{i, b, done}`; `b` = base index excluding retry screens), `stk` (collected word stickers), `res`, `wl` (per-word revision record: box 0–3, due, last…), `look`, `pred`, `test`, `log.d[day] = {s, q, ok}`, `fb` (feedback list), `goal`, `goalDay`, `py`, `auto`, `rs` (reset stamp), `acct` (account code).

Mix/test/rev sessions build their screens at play time (`buildMix`, `buildTest`, `buildRevision`).
- **Two-voice dialogue = `hear` with `lines` (phiếu #13).** `{t:'hear', lines:[{v:'f', zh:'第一句'}, {v:'m', zh:'第二句'}], opts:[right, wrong1, wrong2], whyNot:['', why1, why2], why, w, u}` (instead of `s`). Turn by turn: female = Matcha (key `akey(zh)`), male = Kokoro "D" (`M|akey(zh)`), **one clip per turn**, played in order by `speakLines()` (350 ms gap; tapping 🔊 again or leaving the screen stops the rest). Prompt "Nghe đoạn hội thoại rồi chọn ý đúng:"; "Hiện chữ"/"Pinyin" show the turns marked **Nữ / Nam** (`.dl` + `.dwho`); wrong option → its `whyNot`; answer block (`answerBlock(…, {lines})`) shows each turn on its own line with who speaks, the meaning, 🔊 = the whole exchange, read once automatically. `clipsFor`, `gen_jobs.py`, t_all's clip scan read `lines` with their voice. Used by Bài 2 `TESTBANK.hsk` kind `nghe`.
- **Mixed review `ext` (phiếu #13):** a mix session may carry `ext: [screens]` ("Thẻ mở rộng" cards, ordinary pick/mc): `buildMix` puts them first, not counted in `max`. **Chapter test `TESTBANK.hsk` (phiếu #13):** HSK5-style items with `kind` `nghe` (hear dialogue) / `doc` (mc passage with a blank) / `viet` (order, 完成句子); `buildTest` adds one of each kind, picked at random every time (after question 2, 6 and 10). Bài 1 has none, so its test is unchanged.
- **Chapters (phiếu #9).** `CHAPTERS` (app.src.html) = `{n, zh, vi, ready, loaded, PATH, BOOK, TESTBANK}`; `ready` = Bài 1 or a bundle listed in `CH_SRC` (written by build.py). `loadCh(n)` injects `ch<n>.js`; the bundle calls `regChapter(n, CHn, audio)` (in the standalone file it queues into `window.__pendingCh`, drained at boot). `bootLoad()` (end of `home()`) loads every chapter the learner has started (`st.sess` key `n-…`, a word of that chapter in `st.wl` via `WORD_CH`, or `st.ch`). Session ids: Bài 1 numbers/`m1`/`t1`/`rd`; chapter N `'N-1'`, `'N-m1'`, `'N-g1'`, `'N-t'` — `chOfId()` reads the chapter from the id, `unitNo()` gives the number shown ("tập 1"). `S.ch` is set on every session (REV has 0). `pathOf(n)` = PATH stages with their session; `chSess()` makes the session of a mix/comic/test stage that has no SESSIONS entry once its material exists (comic: every sentence in `ids` has a story picture → pages of ≤ 6 frames by `comicPages()` + a `wrap` that says "Ghép xong truyện!"; test: TESTBANK ≥ 3 tf + 2 spot and all learn stages exist; mix: all its units exist). `STUDY(n)` = playable sessions of chapter n in path order; `STUDY()` = all loaded chapters. `lastLearn(S)` = last learn stage of the chapter's PATH (toast "Xong cả Bài N!"). `bookOf(S)` = that chapter's BOOK (used by read, comic, clipsFor). Test score: Bài 1 `st.test` (unchanged), later chapters `st.tests[id]` (merged by best). Mixed review of a later chapter (`buildMix`): its own words + `ch1` (or ¼ of own) weakest words of earlier chapters already in `st.wl`; with `max` it is `buildShortMix` (match + max−1 questions, older words always asked). Bài 1 mixes are unchanged. DICT is the merged glossary: a taught word of chapter N carries N as 5th item and is underlined as "từ mới" only when `window.CUR_CH` ≥ N (`isT()` in common.js). `hear` shows `whyNot[k]` (Bài 2 content) when option k is chosen wrongly.
- **Revision word choice (phiếu #11).** Cause of the old bug: `buildRevision()` took the 6 words with the highest `pri()`; an overdue word gets 100 + up to 60 points, a word just learned in a unit had `due` = tomorrow (`wrec`) and only got ~25, so the first units always won (and answers inside study units never touch `st.wl`; only reviews/tests call `recordResult`). Now `pickRev(ids, now)`: **đang học** = learned in the last 7 days (`LEARN_WIN`), or box 0–1, or `fail`; **đã vững** = box ≥ 2 and right last time. `learnedAt(id)` = `r.t`, or `st.sess[unit].at` (stamped when a learn unit is finished, since #11) when that is earlier. `dueOf(id)` = `r.due`, but never later than `learnedAt` while the word was never revised (`r.rev` 0) — so it is due the moment its unit is done; old records are only read, never rewritten. A round (`REV_N` = 6): up to `REV_LEARN` = 4 learning words that are due or were wrong (wrong first, then most recently learned), then solid due words (most overdue first), then the rest of the learning ones, then not-due ones by `pri()`. `revInfo()` writes "Lượt này: x từ mới học, y từ cũ" (`.revinfo` on the first screen and in the wrap). `revStatus().due` (the badge on the Ôn tập tổng hợp node) uses `dueOf`. Mixed reviews of the path are unchanged (Bài 1: words of their units + ≤ 3 older ones; Bài 2: + 2 Bài 1 words) and `buildTest`/`buildMix` still rank older words with `pri()`. Test: `tests/t_rev.py` (`REV OK 0 failures`).
- Unlock rule: a unit opens after the previous one is done (`unlocked()`, per chapter PATH); `st.openAll` ("Mở hết các màn") is a developer toggle.
- Retry: `maybeRetry`, set `RT`; `_retry` screens are skipped in results and logging.
- Rendering rules learned the hard way:
  - **Never write a literal CJK range in app source**; use `一-鿿` (the build's validator would choke).
  - `h(tag, cls, html)` uses `innerHTML`. Show Chinese through `zh()` so each word gets ruby `<rt>` pinyin (needed for the 拼 toggle). Where the element's own text is compared (order tiles, spot tiles), keep the plain text in `data-t` — never read `textContent`, it contains the pinyin.
  - **Pinyin of lone 一 / 不 (phiếu #20).** `pySegs(s)` (common.js) cuts a string exactly like `zh()` (longest DICT match; HTML tags such as `<b>` are their own tokens, so they cut words but are looked through for context) and gives each word the pinyin to show: `DICT[k][0]`, except a lone 一 / 不, which `sandhi(k, prevChar, nextChar, nextWord)` changes by the tone of the next word's first syllable as shown. That tone comes from `DT1` = {tone: 'word|word'}, written by build.py (`first_tones()`: the glossary `py:` first syllable, pypinyin when letters differ, neutral → the character's own tone; ~8 KB for all DICT words). `zh()`, `pyL()` (one-line pinyin hints) and `pyOf()` (type-screen hint) all go through `pySegs`, so the tone change shows everywhere; the tap-a-word sheet still shows the dictionary form (一 yī, 不 bù). `t_pinyin.py` runs every chunk of every chapter through the built page's `zh()` and checks lone 一 / 不 against the rules (written there on their own).
  - The tap-a-word sheet is suppressed inside answer areas (`.opt .tile .mw .bigq .wh .sticker .prompt .wcf …`, list in `common.js` click handler) so it never leaks the answer. Add new answer containers to that list.
  - Do not put `${…}` inside a single-quoted JS string (it silently broke the whole page once); use template literals.
  - Audio keys: plain key = female, `M|` = male, `MS|` = male slow; the key is the Han-only text of the sentence (`akey()`).
  - `config.js` must stay a separate file; tests must never use the real API URL (tests serve a blank one).
- Sync design: local-first. `save()` → `syncSoon()` → debounced push to Apps Script (`action push`, optimistic `base` timestamp). A conflict returns the server copy and the client merges **in place** (`mergeInto`: furthest progress per session, union of stickers/feedback, newest per word record, max per day of minutes; the unit currently being played is never touched). Progress reset sets `st.rs`; other devices adopt it only if they have no later activity. Feedback in `st.fb` is copied by the server into the `Feedback` tab. Login: `login` action returns state; a device holding another account's progress is blanked first.
- Apps Script backend (`backend/Code.gs`): tabs `Students` (code, name, active, lastSync, words, unitsDone, minToday, minTotal, streak), `State` (one row per student, JSON split into 8 cells of 44,000 chars each with a leading `~` so Sheets never turns a chunk into a number/formula), `Feedback`. Brute-force guard: 30 wrong codes in 10 minutes locks logins for a few minutes. After editing `Code.gs` Minh must Deploy → Manage deployments → ✏️ → New version.

### 6d. Self-update of the home-screen app (added 2026-10-20)

### 6c. Voice clean-up (2026-10-19)
Minh heard a stray "uh" before and after short male clips (a 2-syllable word sounded like 4). Cause: Kokoro (male) adds low-frequency-only hums at the start/end of short utterances; at speed 0.6 it also invents extra pauses/syllables. Female Matcha clips were always clean. Fix (in `tools/synth.py` + `tools/clipfix.py`): every male WORD clip (key text <= 4 characters, `M|` and `MS|`) is regenerated with `python3 hsk5-src/tools/synth.py --redo-male-words`: multi-syllable words are said 3 times ("X，X，X。") and the middle copy is kept (`cut_middle`); one-syllable words and failures fall back to trimming by 0.9–5 kHz energy (`band_trim`) or the loudest piece between pauses (`main_segment`). The slow clip (`MS|`) is the clean normal clip stretched with ffmpeg `atempo=0.68`, NOT Kokoro's speed 0.6. Result: median 0.36 s per character (female 0.33), 1 segment; slow 0.52 s per character. Full male SENTENCE clips (`M|` longer than 4 characters) were left unchanged; they may still carry a short hum at the end (the 3-copy trick did not work for sentences: lengths went wrong). If Minh hears it in sentences, look for a different method (e.g. forced alignment with an ASR model). New words are cleaned automatically when `synth.py` runs.

**2026-11-07 (phiếu #14): "wu-ơ" — new method, replaces the 19/10 one.** Minh heard an extra "ơ/uh" at the end of some male words (屋 tập 1 Bài 2, Bài 1 too). Causes found: (1) `cut_middle` accepted a piece holding TWO copies whenever Kokoro ran copies together (its length check allowed up to 0.55 s per character): 老婆, 被子, 立刻, 打工 were "老婆老婆"…; (2) `band_trim` (one-syllable words) kept the voiced "ơ" Kokoro adds after an utterance-final syllable — it has energy in 0.9–5 kHz, so the band filter cannot see it (屋, 冻, 夜, 临, 锁, 扑, 轮); (3) 48 male SENTENCES end with an extra syllable the recogniser hears as 啊/了/呢/耶… (…相敬如宾啊).
- **Audit** `tools/audit_voice.py` checks every male clip the content needs (M| and MS|, words and sentences, all chapters; ~1 min). Words: EXTRA = more vowel nuclei than syllables (`clipfix.nuclei`: envelope peaks ≥ 8 dB apart, above −20 dB, centroid < 1.5 kHz), TAIL = ≥ 60 ms above −34 dB after the last syllable has decayed 20 dB, LONG = > 0.46 s per syllable (one syllable: 0.55 s), DOUBLE = the recogniser hears the word twice. Sentences: ASR+ = the recogniser (sherpa-onnx `zipformer-multi-zh-hans-2023-9-2`, offline transducer with timestamps, in `get_models.sh`) hears extra syllables after the last character (0 of 128 female sentences do, so it is reliable). On isolated words the recogniser is NOT reliable (it invents endings for clean female words too, even with a carrier phrase or repeated 3×), so it only gives DOUBLE there. MS| clips that are their M| clip × atempo 0.68 are judged by the M| clip (a stretched long ch/s hiss otherwise looks like an extra nucleus); other MS| clips get SLOW. The male/female length ratio Brain used is printed but is not a flag: Matcha itself ranges 0.20–0.37 s per syllable (一辈子 0.22), so Kokoro words of normal length showed 1.6×. ## 11. CONTENT AND QUESTION PREFERENCES (what Minh has said he likes and dislikes)
