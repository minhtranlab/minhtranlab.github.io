Matrices as transformations: the house explorer

Column 1 of the matrix is where the teal arrow (1, 0) goes: the floor of the house. Column 2 is where the coral arrow (0, 1) goes: the left wall.

Every other point has a fixed address in floor-steps and wall-steps. The roof peak is (0.5, 1.6): half a floor along, then 1.6 walls up. Move the arrows and each point follows its address. That is all M(x, y) computes: x × column 1 + y × column 2.

Drag the round handles at the arrow tips.

Track a point

Things to try:

  • Teal to (0, 1) and coral to (−1, 0): a quarter turn anticlockwise.
  • Teal to (0, 1) and coral to (1, 0): the arrows swap, a reflection in y = x.
  • Teal to (2, 0), coral unchanged: a horizontal stretch, scale factor 2.
  • Turn on the moving grid to see that the whole plane moves, not just the house.
